Split the model generator into dedicated packages. `@kontent-ai/model-generator` now generates only `environment` and `items` models. Delivery SDK, Migration toolkit and Sync SDK models are generated by `@kontent-ai/delivery-sdk-model-generator`, `@kontent-ai/migration-toolkit-model-generator` and `@kontent-ai/sync-sdk-model-generator`, each with its own CLI (`kontent-generate-delivery`, `kontent-generate-migration`, `kontent-generate-sync`).
